### 1. Overview
Diamond Ledger is an **offline-first digital ledger and broker diary** designed for diamond brokers, merchants, and traders. Your privacy is paramount: **Diamond Ledger does not collect, track, upload, or sell any of your personal or financial information.**

---

### 2. Information Collection and Use
- **Zero Personal Data Collection**: We do not collect your name, phone number, contacts, or location.
- **Zero Financial Data Transmission**: All trade entries (Lot No, Selection Weight, Rate, Tums %, Brokerage, Buyer, and Seller) are stored solely on your device.
- **No Cloud Synchronization**: Diamond Ledger operates without an account or cloud backend. Your trade records never leave your phone.

---

### 3. Permissions Requested
- **Storage / File Access (Storage Access Framework)**: Used strictly when you explicitly choose to export your monthly ledger report as an Excel (`.xlsx`) spreadsheet to your local storage or share it via WhatsApp/Email.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services & Advertising
- **No Ads**: Diamond Ledger contains zero advertisements.
- **No Analytics / Trackers**: We do not include Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, or any third-party tracking libraries.

---

### 5. Data Retention & Deletion
You have 100% control over your data:
- To delete entries, you can delete individual records from within the app.
- Clearing the app cache/data or uninstalling the app permanently purges all stored transactions from the device.
