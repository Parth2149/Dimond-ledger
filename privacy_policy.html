Privacy Policy for Diamond Ledger

Effective Date: November 18, 2026 • Version 1.0
Summary: Diamond Ledger is a 100% offline-first application. We do NOT collect, transmit, store, or sell any of your personal or financial data. All diamond trade entries remain strictly confidential and stored exclusively on your local device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Diamond Ledger operates purely as a local offline diary. When using the app:

  • We do not collect personal identity information (such as your name, email, or phone number).
  • We do not collect transaction details, party names, weights, prices, brokerage commissions, or dates.
  • We do not use background location tracking, device fingerprinting, or advertising identifiers.
  • We do not require user registration, cloud login, or account creation.

2. Local Device Storage

All data entered into the application (such as Lot Numbers, Selection Weights, Rates, Tums percentages, and Buyer/Seller names) is saved locally on your device in an isolated Room SQLite database. This data is private and inaccessible to other applications or third parties.

3. Permissions Used by the Application

Diamond Ledger requests only the minimum necessary permissions required for core offline utility:

  • Storage / Document Access (SAF): Used exclusively when you explicitly tap "Export to Excel" to save a .xlsx spreadsheet to your device's download folder or shared storage.

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

Diamond Ledger does not integrate any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking frameworks. No data is ever transmitted to Google, external servers, or third-party marketing companies.

5. Data Backup and Deletion

Because all data resides exclusively on your phone:

  • You retain complete ownership and control over your records at all times.
  • To delete your data, you can delete individual entries within the app, clear app storage via your phone's Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app permanently erases all locally stored records.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application is intended for diamond industry professionals and does not address anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected with a revised "Effective Date" at the top of this page.
